# Content Development Skill

Use this Skill when creating or changing user-visible text, images, labels, empty states, errors, legal pages, or AI-generated content.

## Workflow

```text
Content inventory → Source and owner → Draft → Review → Approval → Publish → Update
```

Before implementation, identify the purpose of each page, the target audience, language and direction, content source, owner, status, and update responsibility. Separate draft content from approved production content.

AI MUST NOT leave lorem ipsum, fake testimonials, fabricated prices, invented claims, dead links, meaningless button labels, or placeholder images in a production release. AI-generated content is untrusted until factual, language, legal, and brand review are complete.

Persian content must be reviewed for natural wording, punctuation, numerals, dates, RTL behavior, and consistency of terminology. Error and empty-state messages must explain the situation and provide a useful next action.

## Completion gate

- [ ] Content inventory exists
- [ ] Source and owner are known
- [ ] Draft/approved/production status is clear
- [ ] Placeholder and fabricated content are removed
- [ ] Persian/RTL and accessibility review is complete where applicable
- [ ] Links, images, labels, and error messages are verified
